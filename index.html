ABOUT ME

Beyond the Code

I'm studying Game Programming at Futuregames in Stockholm and graduate in December 2026. I'm looking for my first role as a junior gameplay programmer.

I've worked on both team projects and solo games. My work includes enemy AI in Unreal Engine, gameplay systems in Unity, and UI and input programming.

I like understanding how things work, especially when something isn't behaving the way I expected. Finding the problem and getting it working is one of my favorite parts of programming.

I'm also a social person. I enjoy working with other people, discussing ideas and helping out wherever I can. For me, making games is just as much about the people I work with as it is about the code.
