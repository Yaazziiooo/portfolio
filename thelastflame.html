The Last Flame

Game Programmer · Unity · C#

Nominated – Best Narrative, Futuregames Awards

About the Game

The Last Flame is a story-driven survival and exploration game set in a frozen, dying world.

Players take on the role of Aurelia, a mystical fox spirit of fire, navigating a desolate landscape while struggling to keep her flame alive.
